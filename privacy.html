Taskly

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Taskly is a personal task and calendar planner that runs entirely in your web browser. This policy explains what information Taskly uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: Taskly has no server and no database. Your data stays in your browser and in your own Google account.

Information Taskly uses

If you sign in with Google and connect your account, Taskly asks your permission to use:

  • Basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) to show who is signed in and to tell your accounts apart.
  • Google Calendar events to display, create, edit and delete events on your calendar.
  • Your list of Google calendars (read-only) to find holiday calendars you already subscribe to, so holidays can be shown.
  • Google Tasks to create, edit, complete and delete the tasks you make in Taskly and to show your existing tasks.
  • Google Drive files you pick (optional, only if you use "Attach from Google Drive"). Taskly can only see the specific files you choose.

You can also use Taskly without connecting Google. In that case, nothing is sent to Google.

How the information is used

Google data is used only to provide the features you see in the app: showing your calendar and tasks, saving changes you make, and keeping Taskly and Google in sync. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Where the information is stored

  • Your tasks, events and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. Taskly does not upload them to any Taskly server, because there isn't one.
  • Google access tokens are kept in memory only while the page is open and are discarded when you close or reload it.
  • Requests to Google's APIs go directly from your browser to Google.
  • Taskly does not use analytics, advertising trackers or its own cookies.

Sharing

Taskly does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and the developer cannot see it. The only place your data travels is between your browser and Google.

Taskly's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load the site, under GitHub's own privacy statement. Google's sign-in script is loaded from Google and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Taskly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect: use the disconnect (×) button next to the Google button in Taskly to stop syncing.
  • Revoke access: remove Taskly at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete local data: clear this site's data in your browser settings. This removes everything Taskly stored on your device.
  • Delete Google data: tasks and events synced to Google live in your Google account. Delete them in Google Calendar or Google Tasks.

Children

Taskly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy
